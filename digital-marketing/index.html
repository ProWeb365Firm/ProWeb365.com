    The Channels We Run and What Each One Is For

    Find the right channel, the ideal scope, and the cost to do it right.

    Local SEO & Google Business Profile

    The single highest-ROI channel for most Minnesota service businesses. We optimize your Google Business Profile (categories, services, attributes, posts, Q&A, photos), fix your citations across every major directory, build out city-specific landing pages, earn local reviews, and structure the site so Google places you in the Map Pack for your highest-intent keywords. Most businesses see ranking and call-volume movement in 60 to 90 days.

    60-90 days to results

    AI Search Optimization (GEO)

    GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is what gets your business cited in the answers AI platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Grok, and Google AI Overviews give. It implements similar signals to classic SEO (authoritative content, structured data, citations) plus a layer of AI-specific tactics, including FAQ schema, entity markup, and AI-citable content blocks. At this time, most businesses in-state are doing none of this work. It’s the largest local marketing opportunity right now.

    Biggest opportunity now

    Content Marketing

    Well-researched articles, service pages, and FAQ content are helpful for visibility in Google and AI search. Content engagement begins with a keyword and buyer-journey map, so each piece of content has a specific job:to earn a ranking, answer a buyer question, generate a lead, or anchor an AI citation. Real content marketing is an investment that compounds over time. Low-quality content fades quickly after it is published.

    4-6 months to traction

    Social Media Marketing & Management

    Social media is an amplifier, not a magic wand. We build a social presence supporting your other channels, like sharing your content, showcasing reviews and customer wins, and humanizing the brand. We focus on the platforms your customers use while treating social engagement as a long-term relationship asset.

    Long-term asset

    Reputation Management

    Reviews are now critical local ranking signals and one of the strongest conversion signals. We install a review request workflow that consistently drives new 5-star reviews. Gain thoughtful keyword-aware responses to every review within 48 hours. We monitor your brand across Google, Facebook, Yelp, BBB, and industry-specific sites to respond quickly to feedback. Most clients double their review count within six months of starting with us.

    2x reviews in 6 months

    Paid Search & Paid Social

    Pay-per-click quickly drives leads. Sometimes it is the right answer, especially when SEO is still ramping up or when you need lead flow this month, not next quarter. We run Google Ads (Search, Local Services, and Performance Max), Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram), and LinkedIn Ads for B2B clients. We tell you when paid is the right channel and when it is just expensive. If your industry has cheap, abundant organic keywords, we will say so.

    Results in weeks

    Email Marketing & Marketing Automation

    Email is the highest-ROI channel that almost no one runs well. We build segmented email lists, set up automated welcome and re-engagement sequences, and tie email tightly to your CRM and lead-capture forms. For e-commerce and high-consideration B2B, email automation alone often returns 30 to 40 times its cost.

    30-40x ROI

    FAQs About Digital Marketing & SEO in Minnesota

    1. How long does digital marketing take to show results?

    This varies on the channel. Paid ads can produce leads within one week. Local SEO typically sees improved ranking and call-volume movement in 60 to 90 days. Content marketing and traditional SEO take 4 to 6 months for steady traction, with full results landing in 6 to 12 months. We will not promise faster results than we can deliver. If a vendor tells you they can rank you in the top five for a competitive keyword in 30 days, run.

    2. What is the difference between SEO and GEO (AI search optimization)?

    Classic SEO ranks your business in Google’s organic results and the Google Map Pack. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) gets your business cited inside the answers ChatGPT, Perplexity, Grok, and Google AI Overviews provide to your customers. The two channels share most of the same underlying signals (authoritative content, structured data, reviews, citations). However, GEO layers on additional tactics like FAQ schema, entity markup, and AI-citable content blocks. In-depth local SEO engagement covers both.

    3. How much should a Minnesota business spend on digital marketing per month?

    A useful rule of thumb is 5%–10% of revenue, weighted higher if you are growing and lower if you are coasting. In dollar amounts, our smallest engagements start around $3,000 per month, with our typical bundled plans running $5,400 per month. Larger multi-location or e-commerce clients often invest $10,800 per month across SEO, content, paid, and email. What matters more than the number is the alignment. Your spend should match the channels that actually move your numbers, not a generic package.

    4. Do you do paid ads, or only SEO and content?

    Both. We run Google Ads (Search, Local Services Ads, and Performance Max), Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram), and LinkedIn Ads for B2B clients. We are honest about when paid is and isn’t the right channel. For some industries, organic compounds are faster and cheaper. For others, paid is the right answer for the first 6–12 months while SEO is warming up. We help you decide.

    5. Do you require a long-term contract?

    No. Our retainers are month-to-month after an initial 90-day commitment. The 90-day window is because effective digital marketing does not produce meaningful results in 30 days. We will not take on an engagement if you expect that. After 90 days, you can cancel with 30 days’ notice. We earn the relationship one month at a time after that.

    6. Can you guarantee specific rankings or leads?

    We will include written performance benchmarks into our agreements where the math supports it. Notably, that is keyword ranking improvements, traffic growth, or lead volume by a specific date. We will not put a guarantee in writing if we have audited your market and do not believe we can hit it. Overpromising is the fastest way to lose a client. Our team would rather lose the sale than the relationship.

    7. Do you only serve the Twin Cities, or all of Minnesota?

    All of Minnesota and out-of-state. Our office is in Roseville, and our clients are across Minneapolis, St. Paul, Rochester, Duluth, St. Cloud, Mankato, Faribault, Owatonna. We service the rest of the state by video, phone, and email. About 30% of our active clients are out of state.

    Week 1: free strategy audit and discovery call.
    Week 2: written 90-day plan, scope, and quote.
    Week 3: signed agreement, kickoff call, access setup, baseline tracking installed.
    Weeks 4–12: execution.
    Month 4 onward: monthly reporting and adjustments. You will know exactly what we are doing each month and exactly what to expect by when.

    9. Will I get a real monthly report?

    Yes. Every retainer client receives a clean monthly report covering keyword rankings (Google organic, Map Pack, and AI), traffic by city and source, phone calls and form submissions, Google Business Profile insights, work completed that month, and next month’s priorities. Zero vanity metrics or fillers.
