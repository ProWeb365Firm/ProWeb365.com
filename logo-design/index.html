    What Makes a Logo Work

    Great logos are not about clever graphics. The best have these five practical characteristics working together:

    It Works Everywhere

    A logo must look professional at every size, from a 16-pixel favicon to a 30-foot building wrap. It has to work in full and single color, on dark and light backgrounds, and in print and on-screen.

    It’s Original

    A logo modified from a stock template is a legal and brand risk. We design every logo from scratch. This leads to original concepts, unmatched artwork, and total ownership forever.

    It Reflects the Business

    Your logo is read by your customers, not by you. We design around your consumers’ decision context. We consider what they need to feel about your business in the first half-second of seeing the logo.

    It Has a System

    A logo is just a symbol that is part of a cohesive brand look. We deliver the logo plus brand essentials: color palette with exact hex/RGB/CMYK values, typography pairing, alternative lockups, and usage guidelines.

    Every File Format

    We send over vector files (AI, EPS, SVG, PDF) that can be scaled infinitely without losing quality, plus optimized raster files (PNG, JPG) for web and social. You will never come back to us for missing file types.

    Common Logo Design Mistakes We Help Clients Avoid

    We see the same mistakes when business owners describe their last logo creation experience.

    Too Literal

    A roofing company logo with a giant roof in it. A bakery logo with a cake on it. Literal logos look amateur and outdated. The best logos are abstract or wordmark-driven.

    Too Trendy

    Logos designed around the visual fashion of the moment look dated within three years. We design for longevity, not for what is hot on Dribble this season.

    Too Complex

    A logo that reads well at 16 pixels (a favicon) and 30 feet (a building wrap) is doing its job. Most amateur logos have too much detail to survive at small sizes.

    Wrong Colors

    Color matters more than most owners realize. Certain color combinations print muddy or look terrible in dark mode. Others have a psychological impact on your customers.

    Wrong Typography

    The font choice in a logo says as much as the icon does. Simple Google Fonts make a brand feel generic. We pair typography intentionally.

    Stock Templates

    A logo modified from a stock template is a legal and brand risk. We design every logo from scratch, including the concept and artwork.

    FAQs About Custom Logo Design Services

    1. How much does logo design cost in Minnesota?

    Our logo design packages start at $950 for Essentials, $2,450 for Standard, and $4,500 for Premium. Most small businesses land on Standard. Larger brand-identity systems with extended deliverables sit at Premium. Logo + website bundles save $1,000–$2,000 over buying each separately.

    2. How long does the logo design process take?

    Most projects are delivered in 3 to 4 weeks. Larger Premium-tier brand-identity systems take 5 to 6 weeks. How quickly you approve direction at each checkpoint impacts deliverability.

    3. Do I own the logo once it's done?

    Yes. Every project ends with a written copyright transfer. You own the design, artwork, files, and rights forever. We never resell or reuse a client logo for someone else.

    4. What file formats do I get?

    Vector files (AI, EPS, SVG, PDF) that scale infinitely without losing quality, plus raster files (PNG with transparency, JPG) optimized for web and social. Color variants include full color, monochrome, reversed (white on dark), and black on white. You will never need to come back to us for a missing file.

    5. Can you redesign my existing logo, or only design new ones?

    Both. About one-third of our logo projects are refreshes or full rebrands of an existing mark. We will tell you honestly whether a refresh (keeping the core idea, modernizing the execution) or a full rebrand (starting over with a new direction) is the right move after we audit your current logo and brand.

    6. Will I see multiple concept directions before picking one?

    Yes. Essentials includes two initial directions; Standard involves five. Each direction is shown with a primary lockup, a monochrome version, and a sample application. You pick the direction that feels right, and we refine from there.

    7. Do you provide a brand style guide?

    Yes. Standard includes a 4-page brand basics PDF covering logo usage, color palette, and typography. Premium includes a full 20+ page brand guidelines document covering extended color systems, typography pairings, icon usage, document templates, and social media templates.

    8. Can you trademark my logo?

    We do not file trademarks, as that is legal work that requires a licensed trademark attorney. We will recommend a Minnesota-based trademark attorney we worked with previously if you want to file. What we do guarantee is that every logo we design is original artwork, so you have a clean trademark foundation.

    9. How do I get started?

Book a free 30-minute video call strategy session to discuss your business and brand goals, look at your current logo if you have one, and recommend the right package with written pricing at no obligation.

    10. Do you offer logo design without web design, or only as part of a website project?

    We offer both. Many clients hire us only for logo and brand identity work. Others bundle logos with a new website. Doing so saves money and produces tighter visual consistency between the brand and site. Either is fine. We will recommend the right approach during your free strategy call.
