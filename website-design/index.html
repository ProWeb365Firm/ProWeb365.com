    Three Things that Together Brings Results!

    ProWeb365 builds websites as part of a complete customer-generating system. When the three components below align, leads pour in. If any are missing, the entire system underperforms.

    A Website Designed for Humans and Built for AI

    Your homepage must communicate your product or service, your target audience, and why a visitor should trust you within the first 3 seconds. Under the surface, the page must be technically structured so Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other AI search engines can read, understand, and recommend your business. We craft copy that converts visitors and add the schema markup, headings, and entity signals, allowing AI search engines to easily understand your web pages.

    Fast, Secure Hosting on Cloud Infrastructure

    Speed is a direct Google ranking signal. Slow sites lose visitors before the first paragraph loads. We host every site built on dedicated cloud servers with daily backups, SSL, an active firewall, and 24/7 monitoring. Sites stay online when a prospect is ready to contact you, not 15 minutes after they’ve already called your competitor.

    FAQs About Minneapolis Website Design in Minnesota

    1. How much does professional Minneapolis web design cost?

    Most of our Minneapolis web design projects run between $4,500 and $15,000+ depending on page count, custom features, e-commerce, and integrations. A simple 5-page small business site lands near the lower end. A custom 11-15 page site with WooCommerce, booking, and ongoing SEO sits at the higher end. We quote in writing after a free strategy call; no surprises, no escalating mid-project.

    2. How long does it take to build my website?

    Most standard websites launch in 4 weeks. Larger e-commerce and custom-feature builds run 6 to 8 weeks. The biggest variable is how quickly you can review and approve content. We give you a written timeline at the start of the project and we hit our deadlines, ask any of our long- term clients.

    3. Will Google and AI search engines like ChatGPT easily index my CMS website?

    Yes, that is the entire point of how we build. Every site we deliver includes on-page SEO, schema markup, AI-friendly content structure, and Google Business Profile alignment so search engines and AI tools can confidently recommend your business. For ongoing ranking growth, we also offer monthly SEO and local marketing packages.

    4. Do you build on WordPress, Shopify, or something else?

    We specialize in WordPress (with the Elementor) and Shopify for e-commerce. WordPress powers more than 70% of websites worldwide for a reason, it is flexible, SEO-friendly, easy for you to update, and far less expensive to maintain than custom-coded sites.

    5. Can I update the website myself after launch?

    Yes. WordPress is built for non-technical owners. After launch we run a personalized training session and send you recorded video walkthroughs you can revisit any time. If you can use email, you can update most of your website content. When you want help, we are an email or call away.

    6. Do you provide hosting, security, and ongoing maintenance?

    Yes. We host every site we build on dedicated cloud infrastructure with SSL, daily backups, a web application firewall, and 24/7 monitoring. We also offer monthly website maintenance services to keep WordPress core, plugins, and themes current and patched.

    7. Do you redesign existing websites or only build new ones?

    Both. About half of our projects are redesigns. If your current site is slow, hard to update, looks dated, or is not bringing in leads, we run a free audit and tell you whether a redesign or a targeted refresh is the right move. We will not sell you a full rebuild if you do not need one.

    8. Do you do e-commerce websites?

    Yes. We build secure, scalable online stores on WooCommerce (inside WordPress) and on Shopify. Both platforms support unlimited products, secure payments, automated tax and shipping, inventory tracking, and customer accounts. We will recommend the right platform after we understand your products and team.

    9. Will you sign a written contract?

    Always. Every project starts with a written service agreement covering scope, deliverables, timeline, payment terms, and what happens if either side wants to make a change. If an agency is unwilling to sign a contract, walk away.

    10. How do we get started?

    Please call (612) 590-8080. We will schedule a free 30-minute strategy call, look at your current site, your top competitors, and the keywords your customers are searching. You will walk away with a written next-step recommendation, whether or not you decide to work with us.
