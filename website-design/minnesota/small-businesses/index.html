    FAQs About Small Business Website Design in Minnesota

    1. How much does a website cost for a small business in Minnesota?

    Most of our small business websites cost between $4,500 and $15,000+. The Starter tier is $4,500–$6,900 for up to 5 pages with custom design, mobile-first layout, basic SEO, and CMS training. The Standard tier is $7,000–$9,900 for up to 10 pages with full SEO and conversion copywriting. The E-Commerce tier is $10,000–$15,000+ for WooCommerce or Shopify, with secure payments, automated tax, and inventory management. Custom builds with advanced integrations run higher. You receive a written quote after a free strategy call.

    2. How long does it take to build a small business website?

    Most standard 7 page small business websites launch in 4 weeks. E-commerce and custom-feature builds run 6 to 8 weeks. The most significant variable is how quickly you can supply content and approve revisions. You will have a written timeline before the project starts.

    3. Will my website be mobile-friendly?

    Yes. We build every site mobile-first, and then scale up for tablet and desktop. Our team benchmarks every site against Google Core Web Vitals before launch. Mobile traffic accounts for over 60% of small business website traffic in Minnesota, so this is non-negotiable.

    4. Can you redesign my existing website?

    Yes. Roughly half of our projects are redesigns. We protect your existing search rankings during the rebuild by mapping every URL, setting up 301 redirects, preserving high-performing content, and submitting the new sitemap to Google on launch day. We run a free audit first and tell you honestly whether a full rebuild or a targeted refresh is the right move.

    5. Will I be able to update the website myself after launch?

    Yes. WordPress with a visual editor lets you update text, swap photos, add blog posts, and change prices without writing a single line of code. We run a personalized training session at handoff and send you recorded video walkthroughs you can revisit whenever.

    6. Do you set up Google Business Profile and local SEO too?

    Baseline on-page local SEO is built into all our sites, including schema markup tying the business to its Minnesota address, city-specific landing pages where appropriate, and Google Business Profile optimization at launch. For ongoing ranking growth (review acquisition, citation building, AI search optimization, monthly content) we offer monthly local SEO packages starting at $3,000/month.

    7. Do you offer hosting and ongoing maintenance?

    Yes. Managed cloud hosting plans start at $95/month with SSL, daily backups, firewall, malware scanning, and 24/7 monitoring. Monthly website maintenance plans start at $250/month and include WordPress updates, content edits, performance monitoring, and security patching. Bundle hosting and maintenance for a small discount.

    8. Can you build an online store for me?

    Yes. We build WooCommerce stores (inside WordPress) and Shopify stores for Minnesota small businesses selling products or services online. Both platforms support unlimited products, secure payments, automated tax and shipping, inventory tracking, customer accounts, and abandoned-cart recovery. Project investment for an e-commerce build begins at $10,000.

    9. What if I am not in the Twin Cities? Do you still serve me?

    Yes. We crafted websites for small businesses in Rochester, Faribault, Owatonna, Mankato, St. Cloud, Duluth, and across rural Minnesota. About 20% of our active clients are out of state. We work by video, phone, and email.. The process is the same as working with us in person.

    10. How do I get started?

    Just call our Minnesota web design company at (612) 590-8080 or use the contact form on this page. We will book a free 30-minute video strategy session, audit your current site (or your competitors if you do not have one yet), and send a written recommendation with scope and pricing at no obligation.
