Why We Build on WordPress (and When We Don't)

Custom WordPress fits the vast majority of Minnesota businesses for five reasons: flexibility, ownership, SEO, low maintenance costs, and ease of in-house updates. Most informational, lead-generation, and small- to mid-sized e-commerce sites function smoothly on its adaptable content management system (CMS) for years. For high-volume e-commerce where product catalogs drive business, Shopify is sometimes a better option. We will tell you which platform suits your business before sending over a quote. Contact our website design services today at (612) 590-8080.

Flexibility

Mold WordPress to fit almost any business model, like service-based businesses, e-commerce, membership sites, directories, booking systems, multi-location offices, and lead generation. No locked-in template, monthly platform tax, or SaaS customization limitations.

Ownership

You own the site, code, content, and database. With proprietary platforms (Wix, Squarespace, GoDaddy Builder, etc.), you rent space inside someone else’s ecosystem. The moment you cancel, the site disappears. WordPress is yours forever.

SEO & AI Search

WordPress is the most search-friendly CMS in the world. Its clean code, lightweight markup, and broad ecosystem of SEO plugins make it simple to optimize for discovery. Add the schema markup, AI-citable content, and on-page structure winning within Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.

Maintenance Cost

Because WordPress is open source, the available expertise is expansive. That lowers the cost of routine maintenance below custom-coded sites. Updates, plugin patches, and security fixes are inexpensive and well-documented.

Easy In-House Updates

If you can use email, you can update most of a properly built WordPress site. We utilize the Elementor visual builder for clients who want a true drag-and-drop edit experience. Our team runs a personal training session at handoff. Handle simple, routine content changes without calling us.

    FAQs About WordPress Website Design

    1. Why should I choose WordPress instead of Wix, Squarespace, or a custom-coded site?

    WordPress gives you ownership, flexibility, and access to the largest plugin and developer ecosystem in the world. It comes at a far lower cost than custom code and without the platform lock-in of Wix, Squarespace, or GoDaddy Builder. The site, database, and content stay yours. If you ever need to move hosts, switch agencies, or sell the business, your website goes with. With proprietary builders (such as Wix, Squarespace, or GoDaddy Builder), you rent space inside someone else’s ecosystem.

    2. How much does a custom WordPress website cost in Minnesota?

    Most of our custom WordPress projects run between $4,500 and $15,000+, depending on page count, custom features, e-commerce, and integrations. A 5-page small business site typically runs $4,500–$6,900. For 8–10 page lead-generation sites containing on-page SEO and content cost $7,000–$9,900. Custom e-commerce, multi-location, or membership sites land higher. Get a written quote after the free strategy call.

    3. How long does a WordPress build take?

    Standard 5 to 8-page business websites launch in 4 weeks. Larger custom or e-commerce builds run 6 to 8 weeks. You receive a written timeline at the start.

    4. Will I be able to update the website myself after it is built?

    Yes. We build with the Elementor visual builder for clients who want drag-and-drop editing. Delivery includes a personalized training session at handoff, plus recorded walkthroughs. If you can use email, you can update most of your WordPress site text and images without calling us.

    5. Is WordPress secure?

    Well-built and maintained WordPress is very secure. Poorly maintained WordPress, notably outdated plugins, weak passwords, or no firewall, is the opposite. Our team hardens every install with security best practices, SSL, an active firewall, malware scanning, and daily backups. Our website maintenance plans keep WordPress core, themes, and plugins current and patched.

    6. Will Google and AI search engines able to index my WordPress site?

    Yes. That is how we build. Every site we deliver includes optimized titles, meta descriptions, H1s, semantic HTML, schema markup, FAQ markup, and AI-citable content structure. WordPress is the most search-friendly CMS in the world. Our on-page SEO is built in by default, not sold as an add-on.

    7. Do you offer hosting and maintenance, or do I have to find that separately?

    We offer managed cloud hosting and monthly maintenance, but neither is required, as you can host wherever you want. Clients who host with us get high-performance cloud servers with SSL, daily backups, an active firewall, malware scanning, and 24/7 monitoring. Most clients choose this option because it is simpler and faster.

    8. Can you redesign my existing WordPress site without losing all of my current Google rankings?

    Yes. Protecting search rankings during a redesign is one of the most overlooked steps in the industry. We map every URL, set up proper 301 redirects, preserve high-performing content, and submit the new sitemap to Google on launch day so rankings carry forward.

    9. Do you build WordPress e-commerce sites?

    Yes. We build on WooCommerce, the leading WordPress e-commerce platform, with support for unlimited products, secure payments, automated tax and shipping, inventory management, customer accounts, and integrations with most major shipping carriers and payment processors. For high-volume product catalogs, we also build on Shopify.
