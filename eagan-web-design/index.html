    Reviews, security badges, and a real phone number visible above the fold, not buried in the footer. Companies based around Eagan know local trends better than mass-market builders.

    Google Reviews

    Look up provider reviews and check how far back consistent good feedback goes. We carry a 4.9-star rating with 40+ verified reviews earned across 16+ years of Minnesota work.

    Proven Track Record

    We have been in business since 2009, working through Google updates, mobile shifts, and the AI evolution.

    Real Listening

    Pay attention to the first conversation. Do they take time to learn about your Eagan business and goals? Do they ask insightful questions before quoting? Do they jump straight to a sales pitch?

    Structured Process

    FAQs About Eagan Web Design, Minnesota

    1. Do you work with Eagan businesses, or only Minneapolis and St. Paul?

    Yes, we actively work with Eagan and the south metro. Many of our Twin Cities clients are in Eagan, Burnsville, Apple Valley, and the surrounding suburbs. Being Roseville-based puts us about 25 minutes from Eagan, close enough that in-person meetings are easy when they help. We also work with plenty of clients entirely by video. Geography is rarely the deciding factor.

    2. How much does an Eagan web design project cost?

    Most ProWeb365 Eagan web design projects fall between $4,500 and $15,000+, depending on page count and features. A clean 3–5 page small business site typically costs $4,500–$6,900. 8–10 page lead-generation sites with on-page SEO and conversion copywriting run $7,000–$9,900. Custom e-commerce, multi-location, or membership sites are priced higher. We send a written quote after a free strategy call, with no hidden fees or surprise pricing later. You own everything we build.

    3. How long does an Eagan web design project take?

    Most standard websites launch in 4 weeks. Larger custom or e-commerce builds run 6 to 8 weeks. The largest variable is how quickly content is approved and how many revisions are requested. You will have a written timeline before the project starts, and updates as we hit our dates.

    4. Can you help Eagan businesses rank in Google across the south metro?

    Yes. We pair Eagan web design with local SEO targeting searches across Eagan, Burnsville, Lakeville, Apple Valley, and the broader south metro. We optimize your Google Business Profile, build out location-relevant content, manage reviews and citations, and track your visibility month over month. Most clients experience Map Pack movement within 60 to 90 days.

    5. Will my new Eagan website be mobile-friendly?

    Yes. Every site we deliver is mobile-first designed, then scaled for tablet and desktop. Mobile traffic accounts for over 60% of small business website searches in Eagan, so this is non-negotiable. We benchmark every site against Google Core Web Vitals before launch.

    6. Can you redesign my existing Eagan website without losing search rankings?

    Yes. Protecting your existing search rankings during a redesign is one of the most overlooked steps in the industry. We map every URL, set up proper 301 redirects, preserve high-performing content, and submit the updated sitemap to Google on launch day. Your rankings carry forward rather than starting from zero.

    7. Do you build e-commerce websites for Eagan businesses?

    Yes. We build secure online stores on WooCommerce (inside WordPress) and Shopify for Eagan businesses that sell products or services online. Both platforms support unlimited products, secure payments, automated tax and shipping, inventory tracking, customer accounts, and abandoned-cart recovery. Project investment for e-commerce typically starts at $10,000 and scales with your product catalog and feature requirements.

    8. Can I update my Eagan website myself after launch?

    Yes. We build on WordPress with the Elementor visual builder so you can update text, swap photos, add blog posts, and change prices without writing a single line of code. We run a hands-on training session at handoff and provide recorded video walkthroughs you can revisit any time. If you can use email, you can run your Eagan website.

    9. Do you provide hosting and ongoing maintenance for Eagan clients?

    Yes. We have been a full-service web design and hosting firm since 2009. Managed cloud hosting plans start at $95/month with SSL, daily backups, firewall, malware scanning, and 24/7 monitoring. Monthly website maintenance plans start at $250/month and cover WordPress core, plugin, and theme updates, content edits, performance monitoring, and security patching. Hosting with the same team that built your site means one phone number to call when something needs fixing.

We will look at your current site (or your top three Eagan competitors if you do not have one yet) and send a written recommendation with scope and pricing. No obligation. You will leave the conversation with a clear, honest read on what your business actually needs next.
