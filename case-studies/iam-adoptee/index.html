FAQs about Non-profit website redesign

1. Our website is old. How do we know if we need a redesign or just updates?

If your site is outdated, difficult to update, doesn’t reflect your current brand, or isn’t mobile-friendly, a full redesign is likely the best next step. Updates can fix small issues, but a redesign addresses core problems with strategy, user experience, and technology, much like we did for the nonprofit featured above.

2. What is the typical cost range for a professional website redesign?

The investment varies based on scope and complexity. For a complete, custom-designed website on a platform like WordPress with training and hosting—similar to this project—a typical range is $3,000 to $7,000. This creates a powerful, long-term asset delivering a strong return on investment.

3. Why did you choose WordPress for this project instead of a simpler builder?

We chose WordPress for its perfect balance of power and simplicity. Unlike closed platforms (e.g., Wix, Squarespace), it offers full ownership, unlimited scalability for growth, and vast customization options. Most importantly, its intuitive dashboard makes it easy for non-technical clients, like the nonprofit team, to be in control.

4. How does premium hosting, like the service you provide, improve our website?

Premium hosting is critical for performance and security. Our hosting solution, as used for this client, ensures faster loading speeds (improving user experience and SEO), provides stronger security against threats, and guarantees higher uptime so a website is always available to your audience.

5. What is the process for a website redesign, and how long does it take?

Our process involves strategy, design, development, and launch phases. For a standard project like the one featured here, the timeline is typically 4 to 8 weeks. This ensures we thoroughly understand your goals, design a tailored solution, and carefully test everything before launching.

6. How do you ensure our team can manage the website after you build it?

Client empowerment is a key part of our service. We don’t simply build and leave -we provide hands-on coaching and clear documentation. As done with this nonprofit—specifically tailored to your team’s needs ensures confidence in making daily updates without any coding knowledge.

7. Do you offer support after our new website launches?

Yes. We offer ongoing support and maintenance plans to provide peace of mind. This can include technical support, regular software updates, and security monitoring to protect your investment and ensure the website continues to perform flawlessly long after launch.
