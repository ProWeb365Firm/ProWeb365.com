      Our Website Design & Development Process

      We’ve refined our process over 16+ years to make creating a new website seamless. A transparent, collaborative approach to creating high-performing WordPress websites.

      WEEK 1

      Strategy & Planning

      We start by diving deep into your business goals and target audience. Our team conducts research on your industry to make informed, strategic recommendations for competitive positioning. Once approved by you, we create a comprehensive project contract outlining timelines, deliverables, and expectations. Everything is transparent and documented before development begins.

      WEEK 2

      Design & Development

      Our team designs and develops your custom WordPress website with a focus on three critical elements: stunning visual design, a user experience (UX) that drives conversions, and strategic search engine optimization (SEO). We engage with you throughout the process, requesting approval at each design milestone before coding. A collaborative approach ensures your vision is conceived as planned.

      WEEK 3

      Testing & Refinement

      We deploy a beta version for thorough testing in a staging environment. Explore every feature, review the content, and provide detailed feedback. We make iterative adjustments based on your input until you are satisfied with the result. Only when you give final approval do we proceed to the launch.

      WEEK 4

      Launch & Long-Term Support

      We coordinate a launch date to take the website live. However, our partnership doesn’t end there. Our team provides ongoing support and maintenance whenever needed. Your success is our success. We’re committed to being your trusted partner as your business grows online.
